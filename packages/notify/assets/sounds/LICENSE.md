# Sounds

`needs-input.wav`, `finished.wav` and `error.wav` are synthesized from scratch by
[`scripts/gen-sounds.mjs`](../../scripts/gen-sounds.mjs) (sine tones with a decay
envelope; no recorded or third-party samples). They are dedicated to the public
domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Regenerate with `npm run sounds -w packages/notify`.
