Phone panel in the dashboard (Q): the pairing QR code right in the terminal (half-block characters, black on white so a phone camera reads it off the monitor), with the link, the mode (this computer / local network / ONLINE through a quick or named tunnel), when the token expires and who is connected; go online / offline, LAN on / off and a new token from the panel, with the cloudflared download and errors shown in it. Going online with O opens it. Its Notifications tab sets up ntfy push in one key (a new random topic, kept in the OS keyring) and shows the QR that subscribes the phone — `ntfy://` for the Android app, the topic's https address and name for an iPhone — then sends a test and shows the last push result. The pairing link and the topic are shown only in this panel.
